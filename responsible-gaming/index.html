Warning Signs

Understanding the signs of problem gambling is crucial. If you experience any of the following, it may be a sign that you should seek help:

  • Chasing losses
  • Increasing bets to recoup losses
  • Playing longer than intended
  • Using money meant for other expenses to gamble
  • Feeling anxious or guilty about your gambling

Deposit and Loss Limits

GT Spins provides tools to help you manage your gambling. You can set deposit limits and loss limits to control your spending and losses. To set these limits, please contact our Support Team.

Self-Exclusion

If you feel the need to take a break from gambling, you can self-exclude yourself from GT Spins. This can be done for a period of 6 months, 1 year, or permanently. Contact our Support Team to initiate the self-exclusion process.

Reality Checks

To help you stay aware of your gambling activity, GT Spins offers reality checks. These prompts remind you of the time spent and money spent while gambling. You can enable these settings in your account preferences.
